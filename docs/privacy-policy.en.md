# G2 Peak View Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

G2 Peak View is an app for Even Realities G2 that shows the ridgeline and mountain
names visible from your location. This policy explains what the app handles and how.

## Permissions the app requests

The app requests exactly two permissions.

### Location

**Purpose**: Only to compute the ridgeline visible from where you are, and the
direction and distance of each mountain.

**Handling**: Your location is **used for computation on your phone only and is never
sent to the developer**. The developer runs no server and does not receive your location.

If your location is unavailable, the app shows a placeholder location (the summit of
Mt. Takao) and says so on screen.

### Network

**Purpose**: Only to download the elevation data needed to compute the ridgeline.

**Destination**: The app connects solely to the elevation tile server of the
Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI), `https://cyberjapandata.gsi.go.jp`.

**What is sent**: The identifiers (tile numbers) of the elevation data being
downloaded. Because the tiles cover roughly 150 km around your location, **GSI's
servers can infer your approximate area**, in the same way as when a map app downloads
map images. Your exact coordinates and anything that identifies you are not sent.
GSI handles its server data under its own policies.

## Data stored on your device

The app does not store your location or settings on your device. Downloaded elevation
data is discarded when the app closes.

## Camera and images

G2 has no camera, and the app never captures, records or sends images of the scenery.
The ridgeline shown is computed from elevation data.

## Sharing with third parties

The app does not sell or share any information with third parties. It shows no ads and
uses no analytics.

## About the data

The app uses GSI's "Elevations of Major Mountains in Japan" and GSI elevation tiles,
processed by the developer. Whether a mountain is visible is estimated from terrain
data only; trees, buildings and weather are not considered. Do not use the app for
route finding or safety decisions in the mountains.
